# Privacy Policy for PakPOS

**Last Updated:** October, 2026

Welcome to **PakPOS: Smart Billing & Khata System** ("PakPOS", "we", "our", or "us"), provided by **Pouyan Creative**. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your data is collected, used, and protected.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use the PakPOS mobile application (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using PakPOS, you consent to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not use the application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, and improve our billing, inventory, and ledger (Khata) services:

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account & Profile Information:** When you register, we may collect your name, business/shop name, phone number, and email address.
- **Business & Transactional Data:** Products, pricing, sales records, invoice logs, customer names, supplier details, and Khata (credit/debit) entries created within the App.
- **Support Requests & Feedback:** Information you provide when contacting our customer support team or submitting inquiries.

### B. Device & Technical Information
- **Device Details:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network information.
- **Log and Usage Data:** Diagnostic logs, crash reports, and performance analytics to ensure application stability.

### C. Device Permissions
To provide core point-of-sale features, PakPOS may request access to:
- **Camera:** Used strictly to scan barcodes and QR codes for quick product lookup and billing. We do not store or transmit your photos or camera feed.
- **Storage / Files:** Used to export and save PDF invoices, receipts, and Excel/CSV backup reports locally on your device.
- **Bluetooth / Nearby Devices:** Used to connect with thermal receipt printers for printing bills and receipts.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- To create, manage, and secure your merchant account.
- To process point-of-sale transactions, generate invoices, and track customer Khata records.
- To synchronize your business records across your devices using secure cloud storage (Firebase).
- To generate business insights, reports, and sales analytics for your shop.
- To detect, prevent, and troubleshoot bugs, technical errors, and security issues.
- To comply with applicable legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

## 4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and administrative safeguards to protect your personal and business data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. 
- All communications between the App and our cloud servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
- Local files and credentials are stored securely on your device using Android system-level sandboxing.

## 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

### A. Retention
We retain your data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the App's services. If you delete your account, your data will be permanently removed or anonymized, except where required by law (e.g., tax or financial record-keeping obligations).

### B. Account & Data Deletion Request
You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated store data at any time:
1. **Online Deletion Form:** You can submit a deletion request via our online web form:  
   👉 **[PakPOS Account Deletion Request Form](https://forms.gle/WNjDEdKN49S5te6B6)**
2. **In-App Option:** You can also request deletion through the in-app settings menu or by contacting our support team directly.

Upon receiving your request, your account credentials, business profiles, and ledger history will be permanently deleted from our servers within **14 to 30 business days**.

## 6. Children's Privacy

PakPOS is designed for business owners, merchants, and professionals. It is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete it.
